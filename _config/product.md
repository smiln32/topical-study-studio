# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Topical Study Studio serves people making complete topical Bible studies for individuals and led classes. They need to define an audience and outcome, choose passages, assemble a coherent set of sessions, adjust every page, and prepare a printed study without manually editing JSON coordinates.

## Product Purpose

Topical Study Studio turns a specification-driven generator into a guided workflow. The study starter lays out one page per passage from a form. The Studio recommends a page blueprint from a library of study patterns and lets the creator add, remove, replace, reorder, and edit pages, with snap-to-grid movement of blocks within validated layouts. Success means a creator can move from a brief to a complete, reviewed, print-ready study while retaining control over every page.

## Positioning

Professional topical Bible study creation without raw JSON or manual coordinates, combining guided structure with creator control.

## Brand Personality

Professional and creative. The interface should feel capable and purposeful while leaving room for thoughtful experimentation and distinctive study work.

## Anti-references

The product must not feel generic. Avoid anonymous AI-generator styling, interchangeable dashboard decoration, unexplained automatic choices, and template-first experiences that erase the creator's judgment.

## Design Principles

- Recommend with reasons; never silently decide the product for the creator.
- Keep the whole study coherent while preserving control over every page.
- Make valid layout behavior visual, immediate, and forgiving.
- Reveal complexity progressively instead of requiring raw coordinates or JSON.
- Treat publication readiness, accessibility, and reader fit as part of creation rather than final cleanup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support complete keyboard operation, strong text and control contrast, visible focus states, screen-reader labels and live status announcements, reduced-motion preferences, and interaction patterns that do not depend on color or pointer precision alone.
